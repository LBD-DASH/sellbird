# 6 Human Needs email rules

Use only these offers, in these words:

- a free 10-minute assessment
- a $29 Personal report
- a $59 Relationship report
- a $149 Personal Report plus 1 hour of coaching

Do not name any other price, discount, guarantee, or result. Do not invent a fact about the reader. If you do not have a fact from the notes, say which offer you are writing about and stop.

Shape:

- Email 1: one plain observation you were given, then one of the four offers as the question. 60 words at most.
- Email 2, 3 days later: a different one of the four offers. 50 words at most.
- Email 3, on day 7 from the first email: one or two sentences, then stop. 30 words at most.
- Someone in South Africa, or a .za address, gets email 1 only.
- No sign-off. Sellbird adds the name, the postal line, and the unsubscribe line.
- Subject: lowercase, 2 to 5 words.
- No dashes used as punctuation, and no exclamation marks.
